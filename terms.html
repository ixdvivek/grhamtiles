Terms & conditions

Last updated July 2026

1. Orders and payment

Orders are confirmed on receipt of a 50% advance. Balance is due before dispatch or on-site laying begins.

2. Handmade variation

Every tile is hand-poured; shade and finish vary naturally between batches. This is expected, not a defect.

3. Lead times

Made-to-order tiles typically take 3–6 weeks depending on quantity and design. We'll confirm a date at order.

4. Shipping and export

Export orders are crated and insured; the buyer is responsible for import duties in the destination country.

5. Cancellations

Custom and made-to-order tiles cannot be cancelled once production has started.

6. Contact

Questions about an order: hello@grhamtiles.com.
