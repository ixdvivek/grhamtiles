Our story

Two years young. One hundred and fifty years of craft.

Grham Home Decors started in Kerala with a simple idea: bring the Athangudi floor — the hand-poured tile tradition of Chettinad, Tamil Nadu — to homes that had never seen it laid. Every tile we make still follows the same eight steps a Chettinad craftsman would recognize. We've just made it easier to bring one home.
